## 1. The market in one line

At 31 December 2025, 103 syndicates were managed by 57 managing agents; 2026 opened with record stamp capacity of about £58.2bn **[likely]**. 2025–2026 saw an unusual wave of ownership change: Zurich–Beazley, Sompo–Aspen, Radian–Inigo, CRC–Atrium and Samsung Fire taking control of Canopius. Ownership change is the single strongest trigger for consulting work.

## 4. First-meeting hypotheses and opening questions

All hypotheses **[guessing]** – test them, never assert them.

| Account | Opening hypothesis | Opening question | Best-fit offers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beazley | Integration choices (platforms, systems, talent) will define 2027 performance | "Which decisions in the Zurich integration matter most for underwriting talent and broker relationships?" | Integration office; platform choice; operating model |
| Canopius | Change of control will trigger a strategy and operating model refresh | "What does the new ownership expect in growth, return on capital and capability?" | Strategy refresh; value model; operations scale-up |
| Hiscox | The challenge is scaling AI beyond one class and keeping governance tight | "What did you learn from the first AI class that changes how you scale?" | AI scaling; governance; evaluation |
| Brit | Group capital and lead franchise in a soft market | "Where are you a lead, and where a follower, in 2027?" | Portfolio and cycle management |
| Ki | Follow performance depends on lead quality and data rights as facilities grow | "How do you test whether the leads you follow are still pricing adequately?" | Lead-quality analytics; model governance |
| Chaucer | Combined ratio moved up about 2.6 points in 2025; margin protection is the agenda | "What drove the 2025 move – mix, rate, or large losses?" | Underwriting performance; benchmark |
| Tokio Marine Kiln / MS Amlin | Group alignment and platform choice | "How does the group decide which risks go through Lloyd's?" | Platform economics; shared services |
| Aspen | Integration into a Japanese group | "Which Aspen capabilities does Sompo want to scale, and which to align?" | Integration; operating model |
| Inigo | A non-insurance parent needs specialty insight and reporting | "What does Radian need to see to back growth?" | Investor reporting; capital efficiency |
| Talbot (AIG) | Portfolio-level AI needs governance and proof | "How will you evidence AI-driven portfolio decisions to Lloyd's and the PRA?" | AI governance; evaluation |
| Markel | Expense ratio (46%) is high relative to claims ratio | "Which parts of the expense base are structural?" | Cost and operating model |
| Lancashire / Hamilton | Soft market plus capital decisions | "Where will you return capital vs deploy it in 2027?" | Capital efficiency; cycle management |
| Ascot / Antares | Capital providers want growth with discipline | "How are you holding rate adequacy while growing capacity?" | Portfolio management; benchmark |

## 5. Account plan template (one page per account)

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Fact base | Owner, syndicates, GWP, combined ratio parts vs peers (from benchmark), class mix, recent news |
| Stakeholders | CEO, CUO, CFO, COO, CRO, Head of DA, CDO – names, priorities, relationships |
| Triggers | Ownership change, results, plan challenges, regulatory findings, technology decisions |
| Hypotheses | Two or three, each with evidence and a test |
| Offer | One diagnostic to propose first (Module 28) |
| Relationship plan | Who you know, who can introduce you, next three touchpoints |
| Proof | Relevant case studies, POV papers, benchmark insight |

---

## 6. Partner's lens

1. **Ownership change is the strongest trigger.** New owners want a fresh, independent view within the first 100 days. Prioritise segment A.
2. **Bring their numbers.** Use the benchmark to open with a peer view – never with a capability slide.
3. **One hypothesis, one question, one offer.** Senior executives give you ten minutes; spend them on the single thing most likely to matter.
4. **Refresh quarterly.** Ownership and leadership are changing fast; a stale profile is worse than none.
