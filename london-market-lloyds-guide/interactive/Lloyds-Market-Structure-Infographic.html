London market field guide · structure map

Lloyd's is a marketplace, not an insurer

Brokers and coverholders bring risk in. Managing agents underwrite it through syndicates. Members provide the capital and carry the risk for their own share. The Corporation of Lloyd's oversees the market.

Tier 2 · Distribution

Two routes into the market

Open market (about 55%)

Lloyd's brokers present each risk to underwriters, often face to face in the Room. A lead sets terms; followers take shares until 100% is placed.

Delegated authority (about 45%)

Coverholders and service companies bind risks locally under a binder. DCAs handle claims under delegated claims authority. They report through bordereaux.

Producing or retail brokers often sit in front of the Lloyd's broker. An MGA may also be a Lloyd's coverholder, but the two terms are not the same.

Tier 3 · Underwriting

Managing agents run syndicates

SYN 1001

Managing agent A employs the underwriters, runs claims and reinsurance, and meets Lloyd's and PRA/FCA rules.

SYN 2002

A syndicate is a pool of members' capital for one year of account. It is not a legal entity.

SYN 3003

One managing agent can run several syndicates, including follow-only and capital-backed ones.

Tier 4 · Capital

Members carry the risk

Corporate members

Companies, mostly owned by insurance groups and investors. Limited liability. The large majority of capital.

Names

Individual members, now mostly through limited-liability vehicles. Advised by members' agents; trade capacity at annual auctions.

Third-party capital

Pension, ILS and private equity money through special purpose arrangements, capital-backed syndicates and London Bridge.

Several, not joint: each member answers only for its own share of each syndicate. Lloyd's Corporation does not carry the risk.

Oversight

Corporation of Lloyd's

  • Council of Lloyd's sets the rules and approves syndicate business plans and capital.
  • Oversight and Performance function (from Nov 2026) joins governance, performance, reserving and syndicate capital setting.
  • Owns the market's licences, brand, Central Fund and Lloyd's Europe (Brussels) for EEA business.
Regulators

PRA and FCA

  • PRA: prudential rules for Lloyd's and managing agents.
  • FCA: conduct of managing agents, brokers and coverholders.
  • Overseas regulators wherever risks are written.
Shared services

Market plumbing

  • Velonetic: premium and claims processing, central settlement (owned by DXC, the IUA and Lloyd's).
  • Lloyd's tools: ATLAS (coverholders), DCOM (binders), Crystal+ (licences and tax).
  • Placing platforms: PPL, Whitespace and others.
  • Standards: MRC contract, Core Data Record (published by LIMOSS), ACORD messages.

Wrong

"Lloyd's insured this risk."

Right

Members backing named syndicates insured it, each for its own share.

Wrong

"The London market is Lloyd's."

Right

The company market sits beside Lloyd's, and both often share one slip.

Wrong

"The lead carries the whole risk."

Right

The lead sets terms. Every participant carries only its signed line.
