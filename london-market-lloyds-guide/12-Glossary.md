# Module 12 – Glossary A–Z

Plain-English definitions of the terms you will hear in London market and Lloyd's work. Cross-references point to the module with more detail.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ACORD** | Global insurance data standards body; London uses ACORD GRLC messages and the CDR. (08) |
| **Acquisition cost** | Brokerage, commission and other costs of obtaining business. (01) |
| **Active Underwriter** | The person who leads a syndicate's underwriting, under the managing agent's governance and Lloyd's rules. (02) |
| **ADC – Adverse development cover** | Reinsurance protecting against reserves developing worse than expected. (07) |
| **Additional premium (AP)** | Extra premium from an endorsement. (04) |
| **Aggregate** | Total exposure or total losses across many policies or a period. |
| **Aggregate limit** | Most the policy or treaty pays over a period or for a coverage, however many losses. (01) |
| **Agreed value** | Policy where the value is fixed at inception (marine, fine art). (15) |
| **Agreement party** | Insurer whose agreement is needed for contract changes or claims under the subscription agreement. (04, 06) |
| **ALAE / ULAE** | Allocated / unallocated loss adjustment expense: claim costs tied to a specific claim (e.g. lawyers) versus general claims department costs. (06) |
| **Aligned capital** | Capital provided by the managing agent's own group. (02) |
| **Annual venture** | Another name for a Lloyd's year of account: each year's underwriting is a separate venture. (21) |
| **Attachment point** | Loss level above which a layer or reinsurance starts to pay. (06) |
| **Average** | Reduction of a claim in proportion to under-insurance. (15) |
| **BAA – Binding authority agreement** | Contract delegating underwriting authority to a coverholder. (05) |
| **Bordereau (plural bordereaux)** | Periodic report of risks, premiums or claims from a coverholder or cedant. (05) |
| **Broker** | Intermediary placing insurance, normally agent of the insured. (02) |
| **Bureau** | Central processing service for premium, claims, accounting and settlement (Velonetic). (06) |
| **Burning cost** | Historical losses ÷ exposure, used for pricing. (17) |
| **Canonical model** | A common data representation used to map data across systems. (08) |
| **Capacity (stamp capacity)** | Maximum premium a syndicate may write in a year of account. (02, 07) |
| **Cat XoL** | Catastrophe excess of loss reinsurance. (03) |
| **CDR – Core Data Record** | Standard set of structured contract data captured at placement for downstream processing. (08) |
| **Cedant** | Insurer buying reinsurance. (03) |
| **Central Fund** | Mutual fund at Lloyd's, the third link of the chain of security. (07) |
| **Chain of security** | Lloyd's three-link capital structure: syndicate assets, Funds at Lloyd's, central assets. (07) |
| **CIDRA** | Consumer Insurance (Disclosure and Representations) Act 2012. (15) |
| **CIL – Coming into Line** | Deadline for members to provide Funds at Lloyd's for the next year. (07) |
| **Claims agreement party** | The party a scheme or contract authorises to agree claims for others (e.g. the lead under CLA or SCAP). (06) |
| **Combined ratio** | (Claims + expenses) ÷ earned premium. (01) |
| **Composite policy** | One policy covering several parties with different interests, treated as separate contracts. (17) |
| **Consortium** | Group of insurers writing together under a consortium leader. (04, 17) |
| **Contra proferentem** | Ambiguous wording interpreted against the party that drafted it. (17) |
| **Contract certainty** | All terms agreed before inception and documented promptly. (04) |
| **Contribution** | Sharing a loss among policies covering the same risk. (15) |
| **Corporate member** | Company providing capital at Lloyd's. (02) |
| **Coverholder** | Firm authorised to bind risks on behalf of Lloyd's syndicates. (05) |
| **Crystal+** | Lloyd's tool (formerly Crystal) for regulatory, licensing and tax information by territory. (04) |
| **Data lineage** | The trace from source data through every change to a report or decision. (08) |
| **Data product** | A governed, reusable data set with an owner, quality standards and service levels. (08) |
| **DCA – Delegated claims administrator** | Third party handling claims on behalf of insurers. (05) |
| **DCOM** | Lloyd's Delegated Contract and Oversight Manager: coverholder onboarding, facility placement and binder registration. A Lloyd's tool, not Velonetic. (05, 24) |
| **Deductible / excess** | Part of the loss paid by the insured. (01) |
| **Delegated authority (DA)** | Insurers giving third parties authority to underwrite or handle claims. (05) |
| **DQ** | Data quality: completeness, accuracy, timeliness, consistency, uniqueness, validity. (08) |
| **Earned premium** | Premium for the expired portion of the policy period. (17) |
| **EBOT** | Electronic Back Office Transactions: ACORD Ruschlikon messages for technical and financial accounts. (06) |
| **ECA – Economic Capital Assessment** | Lloyd's member capital = uSCR × 135%. (07) |
| **ECF – Electronic Claims File** | Market system for sharing and agreeing claims. (06) |
| **ECOT** | Electronic Claims Office Transactions: ACORD Ruschlikon messages for claims movements. (06) |
| **Endorsement** | Change to a bound contract. (04) |
| **EP curve** | Exceedance probability curve – probability of losses exceeding given amounts. (17) |
| **E&S / surplus lines** | US non-admitted insurance market. (01) |
| **Event-driven architecture** | Systems publish lifecycle events (e.g. 'policy bound') that others react to, instead of calling each other directly. (08) |
| **Facultative** | Reinsurance of an individual risk. (03) |
| **Fair presentation** | Insured's pre-contract disclosure duty under the Insurance Act 2015. (15, 16) |
| **FAL – Funds at Lloyd's** | Members' capital supporting underwriting. (07) |
| **FCA** | Financial Conduct Authority – conduct regulator. (07) |
| **FET** | US Federal Excise Tax on premiums paid to non-US insurers. (06) |
| **Financial account** | The cash settlement record that pairs with the technical account. (06) |
| **Firm order** | Broker instruction to proceed on stated terms and lines. (04) |
| **FNOL** | First notification of loss. (06) |
| **Follow market** | Insurers who take a share behind agreed lead terms, with their own line and conditions. (04) |
| **Follower** | Underwriter taking a share on the leader's terms. (04) |
| **FOS** | Financial Ombudsman Service. (15) |
| **Franchise** | Threshold below which nothing is paid; above it, the full loss is paid. (15) |
| **Fronting** | Licensed insurer issues a policy and reinsures the risk to another carrier. (17) |
| **FSCS** | Financial Services Compensation Scheme. (15) |
| **GenAI** | Generative AI: models that produce text, images, code or other content. (09) |
| **GPI – Gross premium income limit** | Maximum premium a coverholder may bind under a BAA. (05) |
| **GUA – General Underwriters' Agreement** | Market agreement defining which changes the leader may agree alone. (04, 16) |
| **Hard market** | Rising prices, tighter terms, scarce capacity. (04) |
| **Hours clause** | Defines the time window that counts as one event for reinsurance. (03) |
| **Human-in-the-loop** | Human review built into an AI workflow. Only a real control if the person has competence, time, information and authority. (09) |
| **IBNR / IBNER** | Incurred but not reported / incurred but not enough reported. (06, 17) |
| **IDP** | Intelligent document processing: extracting structured data from documents. (26) |
| **IFRS 17** | International accounting standard for insurance contracts. (07) |
| **ILS** | Insurance-linked securities, e.g., cat bonds. (03) |
| **Indemnity** | Restoring the insured to the same financial position as before the loss. (15) |
| **Insurable interest** | Legal or financial relationship with the subject matter of insurance. (15) |
| **IPT** | Insurance Premium Tax – 12% standard, 20% higher rate. (16) |
| **IUA** | International Underwriting Association – company market body. (02) |
| **LAE** | Loss adjustment expense: the cost of investigating and settling claims. (06) |
| **Layer** | Band of cover above a retention or lower layer. (01, 17) |
| **Lead market** | Insurer that leads terms and defined placement or claims duties. (04) |
| **Lead underwriter / slip leader** | Underwriter setting terms in a subscription placement. (04) |
| **LIIBA** | London & International Insurance Brokers' Association. (02) |
| **LIMOSS** | London Insurance Market Operations & Strategic Sourcing; owned by the LMA, IUA and Lloyd's. Manages market service contracts and publishes the Market Business Glossary. (02) |
| **Lineslip** | Facility allowing a lead to accept declared risks for all participants. (04, 17) |
| **LLM** | Large language model, the engine behind most GenAI tools. (09) |
| **Lloyd's Claims Lead Arrangements (LCLA)** | Lloyd's rules for agreeing claims on subscription contracts: the lead agrees standard claims; lead and second lead agree complex claims; followers are bound. Replaced the Lloyd's Claims Scheme. (06) |
| **Lloyd's Europe** | Lloyd's Insurance Company S.A., Brussels, for EEA risks. (07) |
| **LMA** | Lloyd's Market Association – represents managing agents. (02) |
| **LMG** | London Market Group – umbrella body of Lloyd's, LMA, IUA and LIIBA. (02) |
| **Loss adjuster / loss assessor** | Expert appointed by insurer / by insured to handle a claim. (06, 16) |
| **LPAN** | Premium advice note submitted by the broker to the bureau. (06) |
| **LPT – Loss portfolio transfer** | Transfer of existing claims liabilities to a reinsurer. (07) |
| **Managing agent** | Company managing syndicates on behalf of members. (02) |
| **Master policy** | One policy for an organisation with certificates to members. (17) |
| **MBG** | Market Business Glossary: LIMOSS portal holding the CDR, market definitions and reference data. (08) |
| **MCR** | Minimum Capital Requirement under Solvency II/UK. (16) |
| **MDM** | Master data management: keeping one trusted record for entities such as insureds and brokers. (08) |
| **MGA** | Managing General Agent – binds business for insurers. (05) |
| **Model drift** | A model's accuracy changes over time because the data or relationships change. (09) |
| **Moral hazard** | Increase in risk from people's behaviour or attitude. (15) |
| **MRC – Market Reform Contract** | Standard London placing and contract document. (04) |
| **Names** | Individual members of Lloyd's. (02) |
| **NIGO** | 'Not in good order': a submission or transaction with missing or invalid information. (11) |
| **Non-bureau** | Business processed directly between broker and insurer. (06) |
| **Occurrence / event** | How losses are grouped under a policy or treaty, often with an hours clause for catastrophes. (06) |
| **Open year** | Lloyd's year of account not closed at 36 months. (07) |
| **Order hereon** | The share of the whole risk being placed on this slip (e.g. 60% of 100%). Not 'the remaining share'. (04) |
| **OSND / SND** | (Original) Signing Number and Date – bureau reference for accounting entries. (06) |
| **Peril / hazard** | Cause of loss / feature increasing likelihood or size of loss. (15) |
| **PML** | Probable maximum loss. (04) |
| **PPL** | Placing Platform Limited – electronic placing platform. (02) |
| **PRA** | Prudential Regulation Authority – prudential regulator. (07) |
| **Premium trust fund (PTF)** | Trust funds holding Lloyd's syndicate premiums. (06) |
| **Prior submit** | Delegated arrangement where the coverholder must get insurer approval before binding. (05) |
| **Proportional / non-proportional** | Reinsurance sharing premium and losses / paying above a retention. (03) |
| **Proximate cause** | Dominant, effective cause of loss. (15) |
| **PS25/21** | FCA policy statement (Dec 2025) simplifying insurance rules for commercial customers. (07) |
| **Quota share** | Proportional treaty ceding a fixed percentage. (03, 16) |
| **RARC** | Risk-adjusted rate change. (04) |
| **RDS – Realistic Disaster Scenario** | Lloyd's prescribed catastrophe "what if" events. (17) |
| **Reinstatement** | Restoration of a reinsurance limit after a loss, often for extra premium. (16) |
| **Retrocession** | Reinsurance of reinsurers. (01) |
| **Return premium (RP)** | Premium returned on endorsement or cancellation. (04) |
| **RFI** | Request for information from underwriter to broker. (04) |
| **Risk code** | Lloyd's classification code for business. (08) |
| **RITC – Reinsurance to Close** | Transfer of a closing year of account's liabilities to a later year/syndicate. (07) |
| **ROL – Rate on line** | Reinsurance premium ÷ limit. (16) |
| **SBF – Syndicate Business Forecast** | Annual syndicate business plan submitted to Lloyd's. (07, 17) |
| **SCAP** | Single Claims Agreement Party: slip leader agrees non-complex claims up to £250,000 for all participants who adopted it at placement. (06) |
| **SCR / uSCR** | Solvency Capital Requirement (1-year) / ultimate SCR (to ultimate). (07) |
| **Service company** | Group-owned coverholder of a managing agent. (02, 05) |
| **Several liability** | Each insurer liable only for its own share. (04) |
| **Side letter** | Separate document varying a contract – a contract certainty risk. (17) |
| **SIEM** | Security information and event management: collects and analyses security logs. (24) |
| **Signed line / written line** | Final share after signing down / share written by the underwriter. (04) |
| **SM&CR** | Senior Managers & Certification Regime. (07, 16) |
| **Soft market** | Falling prices, wider terms, plentiful capacity. (04) |
| **SOV – Statement of values** | Schedule of insured locations and values. (03) |
| **SPA – Special purpose arrangement** | Syndicate writing a quota share of a host syndicate. (02) |
| **Subjectivity** | A condition or evidence required under the contract, with a due date and a consequence if not met. (04) |
| **Subrogation** | Insurer's right to recover from a responsible third party after paying. (15) |
| **Subscription market** | Market where many insurers share one risk. (01, 04) |
| **Surplus treaty** | Proportional treaty ceding amounts above the cedant's retention, in lines. (16) |
| **Syndicate** | One or more members underwriting at Lloyd's; not a legal entity. (02) |
| **System of record** | The master application for a business object. (24) |
| **Technical account** | Part of an insurer's income statement showing underwriting results. (17) |
| **TOBA** | Terms of Business Agreement between broker and insurer or client. (16) |
| **TPA** | Third-party administrator (claims or policy administration). (05) |
| **Treaty** | Reinsurance covering a whole portfolio. (03) |
| **UCR** | Unique Claim Reference. (06) |
| **UMR** | Unique Market Reference for a contract. (04) |
| **Unearned premium** | Premium for the unexpired part of the policy period. (17) |
| **Velonetic** | Bureau services provider for the London market (part of DXC). (02) |
| **Warranty** | Insured's promise; breach suspends cover under the Insurance Act 2015. (16) |
| **Whitespace** | Electronic placing platform used in London. (02) |
| **Workflow orchestration** | Coordinating tasks, state, rules, tools, approvals and exceptions across systems. (26) |
| **XoL – Excess of loss** | Non-proportional reinsurance paying above a retention. (03) |
| **Year of account (YOA)** | All Lloyd's policies incepting in a calendar year, accounted as one venture. (07) |
